# Rahu: The Endless Hunger for More

Rahu is one of the most powerful factors in the astrology field as it shows desire without any bounds. Unlike most planets Rahu is not satisfied with what he has. It grows and proliferates all it comes into contact with, be it ambition, riches, glory, knowledge or influence. This trait is the cause of numerous extraordinary feats as well as why people with a strong Rahu desire something that is missing even after achieving what most people want to. **Rahu is like a mouth that has no body, it eats and eats but doesn't seem to get full**, the lecture said. This is the reason why success is usually not enough for Rahu. Each success is just a new wish.

## Ambition and the Break from Convention

### Authority in Unusual Ways

Rahu is constantly trying to get authority and leadership in unusual ways, unlike the Sun which is an authority and leader by nature. Is not fond of working in a traditional system or traditional methods. Rahu, on the other hand, challenges the status quo and looks for alternative options. Takes pleasure in making something new instead of something that is the same. That's why **Rahu is often linked to innovators, entrepreneurs, scientists, researchers, political strategists and people who question the status quo.** Questions should not be based on "How is this usually done?". Rahu says, "Is there another way? This attitude sometimes can lead to incredible finds, but can also result in an unwillingness to accept advice just because it seems "conventional".

### A Symbol of Modernity

The lecture goes back to the same theme of Rahu as a symbol of modernity over and over again. Anything that is unconventional in nature is under Rahu's sphere. From technology, AI, digital communication, social media, networking, globalization, modern entertainment, and unconventional lifestyles, all of these embody Rahu's drive to transcend the confines of the known. **Ketu is always finding its roots and Rahu is always searching for future of a system.** It's always looking for the next opportunity and that's why it's attracted to innovation. That's why people under Rahu's influence are quick to learn new technologies and easily enjoy experimenting with new concepts before anyone else does.

## Communication and Influence

### The Gift of Networking

Rahu's greatest strength is that it can communicate with virtually anyone. Is not easily discerning between social classes, occupations, or cultures. Rather, it sees every encounter as a chance. **Rahu can thus acquire the skill of networking as one of his natural skills.** A Rahu house man is comfortable in talking to strangers, establishing business relationship and increasing socialization. It is this quality which is useful especially in the fields of Public influence, diplomacy, media, business development, politics and marketing. The lecture also states, however, that Rahu can talk a lot. It loves to explain, provide examples, and provide layers of detail – sometimes too much detail, too much explanation, and too much out of the way detail around a simple idea.

### When Expansion Turns Poisonous

Rahu has the greatest gift of expansion and the greatest weakness of expansion. As Rahu drifts through different planets, the dose of moderation gradually diminishes. A healthy ambition can develop into an addiction. Arrogance can be a result of confidence. The quest for knowledge can become excessive contemplation. Love can turn into an unhealthy attachment. If Rahu keeps going, he can get carried away even with knowledge. This tendency is contrasted with the inflammatory process in the body during the lecture. As body growth can become detrimental to the body at times, **Rahu's psychological growth can also become poisonous at some stage.** This is not to say Rahu is a bad planet or sign, but rather that each strength can be used in a way that is destructive when it is overused.

### Hidden Authority

The other idea which is discussed in the lecture is very interesting-Rahu's relation with hidden authority. Rahu is said to work behind the scenes and not openly exercise power. There are many occasions when it seems as though there is one leader on the surface, but another is working just behind the scenes. This hidden influence is symbolic of Rahu. **Rahu's indirect influence is seen in the political advisors, intelligence bureaus, secret strategists, parallel governments and people who have influence without being known in the public eye.** Rahu can also symbolize the unspoken voice in a family or organization that makes an impact on all decisions, but is not usually in the limelight.

## Career and Relationships

### Career: No Room for Routine

The lecture also informs you that Rahu hates the regular professions. People under the influence of Rahu tend to face difficulties when it comes to repetitive work or a strict schedule as they would rather be free to experiment. Rahu wants to be in a job where he is original and innovative and thinks independently. Rahu often pushes for professions that are out of the norm, are unconventional or are ahead of their time when placed in the tenth house or with the tenth house lord. Haru does not settle inside any pre-established structures, on the contrary, he loves to establish a new one. Thus, it is not unusual to see that **startup founders, digital creators, researchers, consultants, entrepreneurs, and innovators often have a strong Rahu influence.** They don't like to sit on their laurels; they always try to improve or change things.

### Relationships: Rahu with Venus

Rahu's relationship also intensifies to a greater degree than usual. The conjunction of Rahu with Venus is emphasized especially in the lecture. These pairings can lead to an extraordinary need for love, beauty, luxury, artistic expression and growth in financial capital. The placement can, however, create unrealistic expectations, emotional attachments, multiple relationships or excessive attachments. Rahu brings in the attraction and illusion. This can lead people in idealizing the relationships they want to have instead of making do with what they have. The notes indicate that after getting what it wanted, **loyalty and emotional stability begin to become important remedies** since Rahu's nature is that of always needing more.

## The Karmic Meaning

Last but not least, one of Rahu's karmic meanings is explained in the lecture. **Rahu is the desire that is not satiated in the previous lives.** Its location in the horoscope represents the part of life where the soul keeps looking for fulfilment. Why people seem to gravitate toward the things Rahu's house indicates is because they are their normal inclinations.

## Example

Suppose two businessmen started their businesses on the same day. The first creates a solid business, makes a good living and is content with slow and steady growth. The second entrepreneur is always looking for bigger markets, more investors, more recognition and international expansion. After getting to a point of success, they think immediately about the next step to take, as the previous one isn't enough. **This second approach is due to Rahu's nature.** It continually drives the person forward to new and higher achievements, but does not give them the chance to stand back and take stock of what they have accomplished.
