## What Is a Birth Chart?

A birth chart — also called a natal chart — is a map of the sky at the exact moment of your birth. It shows the positions of the Sun, Moon, and planets against the backdrop of the zodiac, divided into twelve houses.

No two birth charts are identical. Even twins born minutes apart can have meaningfully different charts. This makes it one of the most personal tools in astrology.

## The Three Pillars

Every chart reading rests on three fundamentals:

### Planets

Planets represent different facets of the psyche and life experience. The Sun points to identity and purpose; the Moon reflects emotional patterns and instinct; Mars governs action and desire.

### Zodiac Signs

The sign a planet occupies colours how that planet expresses itself. Mars in Cancer acts very differently from Mars in Aries — same drive, different channel.

### Houses

The twelve houses map the planet's energy onto a specific life domain — relationships, career, family, spirituality, and so on. The house system depends on your birth time and place, which is why an accurate birth time matters.

## Where to Begin

Start with the three most visible layers of your chart:

1. **Sun sign** — your core sense of self and purpose
2. **Moon sign** — your emotional baseline and needs
3. **Rising sign (Ascendant)** — the mask you show the world, and how others first perceive you

These three together are often called the "big three" and give a rounded first impression of the chart before diving deeper into aspects and house placements.

## A Note on Interpretation

Astrology is a symbolic language, not a deterministic system. A chart shows tendencies and patterns — it does not dictate outcomes. The same Saturn in the seventh house can manifest as disciplined partnerships or persistent difficulties in relationships, depending on how consciously the person engages with the energy.

Approach your chart with curiosity rather than anxiety. The goal is self-understanding, not prediction.
