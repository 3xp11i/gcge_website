# Understanding Ketu: The Headless Wisdom

Ketu is a difficult concept to grasp as its outcomes are seldom good in the initial phase. The Rahu continuously moves towards getting more and more; on the contrary, Ketu pulls a person away from the things he/she has already attained. The lecture states that **Ketu corresponds to the things which the soul has already learnt in the previous births.** Due to this previous experience, it creates less excitement towards these areas and instead encourages detachment. Ketu is depicted as a body devoid of a head, indicating that it is not a place of logic or continual analysis. It works by **instinct, intuition and a sort of unconscious knowledge** of the situation, rather. That's why when it comes to knowing something, people with a strong Ketu influence are unable to articulate how they know it.

## Nature and Inclinations

### Silence Over Recognition

Ketu is in opposition to Rahu, who wants attention and recognition, and enjoys silence. It likes to meditate, think, be alone, do yoga, and to study things that are too deep and not popular. The lecture doesn't equate Ketu with classical music, instrumental music, spirituality or the origin of knowledge; it's more about the former. Ketu isn't about looking good in front of the crowd, it's about finding truth behind the looks. This is the reason why **Ketu's natural inclination is to excel in astrology, medicine, occult sciences, investigation, archaeology and deep research.** Ketu loves to learn the detailed knowledge of a single subject rather than a general knowledge of many subjects.

### The Lesson of Detachment

An experience of detachment is one of the toughest lessons Ketu has to learn. From wherever Ketu is placed, it might initially provide positive experiences but one after the other, it shows their constraints. The lecture clarifies that **Ketu may bring joy in a specific field initially, and then inflict sorrow later for the person**, compelling them to release their unhealthy attachment. Ketu's relationship with Moon, for instance, may indicate that the child was blessed with a lot of love from the mother, but it was then transformed into a cold or difficult relationship. Likewise, Ketu with siblings or a romantic passion leads the person to give generously and to learn an important lesson when they are betrayed or separated as a result of their giving. These experiences are offered as **opportunities for inner growth, rather than as punishment.**

## Communication and Relationships

### Communication Style

Ketu also alters the communication style of the person. Rahu likes to elaborate on things, Ketu doesn't like to say much. The person may not finish the conversation as they think the others should know what they are thinking. Sometimes Ketu will be exceptionally silent, unless the topic is one which it is fond of. When this occurs, however, he or she can talk for hours about very minute details with great depth. Ketu's selective communication is indicative of his distance, or lack of interest in what is happening, that does not entail the unnecessary use of energy on topics that are of no real interest.

### Ketu and Jupiter

The other interesting point to be made in the lecture is on the correlation of Ketu and Jupiter. In itself, **Ketu is said to be un-directed.** It gets no ambition nor ego and requires Jupiter's wisdom to direct it on the right path. In Jupiter's influence over Ketu, many of its bad influences find their balance and intuition becomes true wisdom. In the absence of Jupiter, however, Ketu can cause confusion, isolation or directionless detachment. This symbolic connection is one of the major reasons for Jupiter's importance during spiritual development.

### Loyalty

Ketu's loyalty is also a highlight of the lecture. While Rahu is always looking for new experiences, **Ketu once it makes a real connection sticks with it.** Compared to dogs because they are loyal and able to love even in times of adversity. But loyalty can only take it so far. If Ketu makes the mistake more than a few times, then it can forget the relationship and move on without looking back. There is little drama in confrontation. Rather, Ketu strolls away, while all the talk fades away.

## Education, Career and the Deeper Lesson

### Education and Career

Ketu also affects one's education and career. In conjunction with Mercury, it can result in gaps in learning, disinterest in regular classes or picking out highly specialized topics. Inserting a subject or a story that is not connecting with the person's personal life, may lead the person to give it up eventually. But the same spot can give rise to incredible scientists, astrologists, physicians or scholars who specialize in a single study. **Ketu does not refuse knowledge, it denies knowledge without a purpose.**

### Letting Go, Not Taking

One of the most crucial karmic lessons given in the lecture is that **Ketu is a symbol of letting go, not taking.** Rahu gives knowledge through desire while Ketu gives knowledge through surrender. The two paths take opposite directions and both lead to the same end. Rahu states that "one must taste everything until one understands that it will not suffice. Ketu reminds us to "let go of what no longer lifts you higher. They both make up the karmic journey.

## Rahu vs. Ketu: A Quick Comparison

| Aspect | Rahu | Ketu |
|---|---|---|
| Direction of pull | Moves towards getting more and more | Pulls away from what has already been attained |
| Source of knowledge | Knowledge through desire | Knowledge through surrender |
| Communication | Elaborates on things | Doesn't like to say much |
| Social orientation | Wants attention and recognition | Enjoys silence |
| Learning style | General knowledge of many subjects | Detailed knowledge of a single subject |
| Needs guidance from | — | Jupiter, to direct its intuition into wisdom |
| Approach to connection | Always looking for new experiences | Sticks with a real connection once made |

## Example

Suppose that two artists are equally talented. The first one is always looking to win awards, get likes on social media, be shown at exhibitions, receive public recognition. Each success spurs them on towards a still higher goal. This second artist slowly becomes uninterested in the glamour of it and starts painting for him or herself. It doesn't matter whether anyone knows or not for the joy is in the process itself. **The first one is Rahu's continuous strive for outer achievements, and the latter is Ketu's journey towards inner fulfilment.** Both paths are valid and correct, it is just that they are two different side-by-side sides of karmic evolution, as explained in the lecture.
